Step-by-Step Excellence

The Manufacturing Workflow

From graded raw material batching to final hydraulic compaction and scientific humidity-controlled curing.

STAGE 01

Automated Batching & Weighing

Computerized micro-weighing of 53-Grade OPC cement, washed silica sands, and basalt gravel to ensure absolute consistency across every single batch.

STAGE 02

Intensive Planetary Mixing

High-torque planetary pan mixers blend the dry aggregates with synthetic German iron-oxide pigments, ensuring UV-stable, deeply saturated colors.

STAGE 03

120-Ton Hydraulic Vibro-Pressing

Simultaneous multi-directional high-frequency vibration and 120-ton hydraulic down-pressure eliminate micro-voids, producing rock-solid density.

STAGE 04

Controlled Steam & Humidity Curing

Automated curing chambers maintain optimal humidity and temperature for complete cement hydration, reaching peak design strength without surface cracking.

STAGE 05

In-House Lab Strength Testing

Random sample blocks from every production cycle undergo 28-day digital compressive testing (CTM), water immersion, and abrasion index validation.

STAGE 06

Palletization & Fleet Dispatch

Blocks are machine-stacked on heavy-duty wooden pallets, stretch-wrapped for zero transport damage, and loaded onto our dedicated logistics fleet.

Premium Raw Materials

Quality Starts with Uncompromising Sourcing

The strength of precast concrete lies in the purity of its inputs. Shiv Traders sources only certified high-performance raw materials to ensure every paver exceeds CPWD and BIS benchmarks.

  • Ultra 53-Grade OPC Cement: High early strength and low alkali content for durability.
  • Crushed Basalt Quartz Aggregates: Flakiness & elongation index strictly below 15%.
  • Washed River Silica Sands: Free from silt, clay, and organic impurities.
  • German Synthetic Pigments: Non-fading, UV-resistant inorganic iron oxides.
