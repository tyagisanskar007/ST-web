Sub-base Engineering

Why Foundations Matter

An interlocking paver block pavement is only as strong as the sub-base beneath it.

1. Compacted Sub-grade

Heavy vibratory rolling to achieve 98% Modified Proctor Density (MDD), preventing long-term pavement rutting.

2. Base Course (WMM / GSB)

Granular stone aggregates graded to provide water drainage and uniform load distribution under wheel axles.

3. Interlocked Surface & Edge Kerb

Rigid kerb confinement prevents lateral block shifting, locking the entire surface into a monolithic load-bearing carpet.
